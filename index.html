Welcome

A Message from
Our Founder

My name is Fawnia, and I’ve seen firsthand what cancer can do, and how deeply it can impact the lives of those we love. I believe we can do more to help people build stronger, healthier lives and give their bodies the best possible foundation before, during and after cancer treatment.

The Melissa Foundation was created from that belief: to empower people with knowledge, resources and support to make healthier choices and live healthier lives at every stage of their journey.
