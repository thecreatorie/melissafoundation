Your Gift Helps People Live Healthier

The Melissa Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Every donation goes directly toward helping people find trusted resources, make healthier choices, and feel supported at every stage of their journey — before, during and after cancer.

  • Sharing trusted resources and practitioner referrals
  • Hosting community healing events like yoga and sound healing
  • Creating recipes and educational content on healthy living
  • Honoring Jamilla’s memory by helping others

Thank you for turning loss into hope, and knowledge into action.
